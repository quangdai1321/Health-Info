Now the limitations. I want to state them clearly.
First, no clinical validation. All our evaluation is automated.
No medical professional has reviewed the explanations.
Second, our severity thresholds are heuristic. They do not come from a laboratory critical value list.
Third, the data is synthetic. Real reports have noise, missing fields, and different formats.
Fourth, the scope is narrow. We support the complete blood count only.
No biochemistry, no qualitative results, and no image input.

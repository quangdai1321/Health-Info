First, false reassurance deserves its own metric.
But a falsely reassuring sentence can score high on readability, because it is simple and calming.
The harm is that it stops the reader from seeking care.
Second, unit standardisation is a safety precondition.
Our unit error happened inside the rule layer. The layer we treat as exact.
No later check can detect it, because every later layer repeats a confidently wrong label.
So standards, like LOINC and HL7 FHIR, are needed, before we build rule-based clinical logic.

A patient leaves the laboratory with a page of numbers.
The page is full of strange terms. P D W. M C H C. Monocyte.
And strange units. G per litre. Femtolitre. Picogram.
The reference interval is printed beside each value.
So the patient can see which number is outside the range.
But they cannot see what the analyte measures.
They cannot see if the deviation is serious. And they do not know what to do next.
Health literacy in Vietnam is still limited.
In a study in Da Nang, with two hundred and four older adults, sixty point three percent had inadequate health literacy.
And in another study, forty-six percent of patients searched online, to understand their own results.

Our paper has four contributions.
First, a four-layer pipeline.
All clinical judgement is completed by rules, before the model is called. The model only paraphrases.
Second, an automatic validator.
Third, the false reassurance metric.
Earlier work on patient-facing explanations did not report this error separately.
Fourth, a finding about units.
Inconsistent units between laboratories caused a severe misclassification, inside our rule layer.

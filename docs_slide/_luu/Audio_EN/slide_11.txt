Now the evaluation.
We used one hundred and fifty synthetic blood count panels. Each panel has ten to eighteen analytes.
The panels are split equally across the three severity classes.
Names and units are random, to test layer one.
Why synthetic data? There are three reasons.
We need ground truth labels, to score the output. Real reports do not carry them.
We need a balanced design. Real panels are mostly normal, so we would have too few severe cases.
And this way we do not handle patient data.
We compare three configurations.
Baseline gives the raw values to the model. No labels, no constraints, no validation.
This is what happens when a patient pastes a report into a chatbot.
Constrained uses layers one to three. Labels and constraints, but no validation.
Full uses all four layers.

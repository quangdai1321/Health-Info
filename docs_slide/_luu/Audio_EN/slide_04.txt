So what do patients do? They photograph the report, and they ask a chatbot.
We tried this with our own model. We asked, in Vietnamese: please explain what white blood cells are.
The model answered: white blood cells are lymphocytes.
That is a definitional error. Lymphocytes are only one type of white blood cell. They are not a synonym.
The model also said white blood cells fight cancer cells. And it mentioned a weakened immune system.
Nobody asked about cancer.
So a patient with a completely normal result hears the word cancer, in one short answer.
